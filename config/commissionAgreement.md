# Commission agreement

Between Valet Network, Inc. and an independent sales representative.

This agreement is between Valet Network, Inc., 21-31 24th Street, Astoria, NY 11105 ("Valet Network"), and {REP_NAME}, {REP_PHONE}, {REP_EMAIL} ("the Rep"). It starts on {START_DATE}, the day the Rep signs it.

## 1. Appointment

- Valet Network authorizes the Rep to sell its Permanent valet subscription (a "Subscription") to customers in New York City, on Valet Network's behalf, on the terms in this agreement.
- The Rep may sell to residents, buildings, brokers, businesses and any other person who will use the Subscription.
- The appointment is non-exclusive. There is no territory, quota, minimum number of Subscriptions or set hours. Valet Network may appoint other representatives and sell directly. The Rep may work for others.

## 2. Price

- Valet Network sets the price of a Subscription for each neighborhood and publishes it at valetnetwork.co/pricing, in the Price column. A customer's neighborhood is the one Valet Network's system assigns to the customer's pick-up and drop-off address.
- The Rep sells at the published price only. The Rep does not offer a discount, credit, free time or any term that is not in Valet Network's customer terms, unless Valet Network approves it in writing first.

## 3. Commission

- For each Subscription credited to the Rep under section 4, Valet Network pays the Rep the monthly commission published for the customer's neighborhood at valetnetwork.co/pricing, in the Commission column, on the day the Subscription is created. That amount is fixed for the life of that Subscription, including if the customer later moves to another neighborhood.
- The commission is earned for each calendar month in which the Subscription is active and the customer has paid for it, including the first and the last such month. It continues for as long as the Subscription stays active, including after this agreement ends, except as set out in section 9.
- No commission is earned for time the customer has not paid for: free trials, free or pilot periods, time refunded or credited to the customer, and unpaid invoices. When an unpaid invoice is later paid, the commission for that month is earned then.
- Valet Network may change the published commission amounts for future Subscriptions by giving the Rep at least 30 days' notice by email. A change never reduces the commission on a Subscription created before the change takes effect.

## 4. Which Subscriptions are the Rep's

- A Subscription is credited to the Rep when the customer subscribes through an invitation the Rep created in the rep portal at valetnetwork.co/rep, or enters the Rep's code (the Rep's phone number) when subscribing.
- A Subscription is credited to one representative only. If more than one representative's invitation or code could apply, it is credited to the representative whose invitation the customer accepted or whose code the customer entered.
- The rep portal shows the Rep every Subscription credited to them, its status and what it has earned. If the Rep believes a Subscription is missing or wrong, the Rep tells Valet Network by email within 60 days of the statement it should have appeared on. Valet Network replies by email within 15 days with its records, and pays any amount owed with the next payment.

## 5. Bonus

- Valet Network pays the Rep $100 for each Subscription credited to the Rep when that Subscription completes its first full paid month, one month after the customer's first paid day. Free and pilot periods do not count toward that month.
- No bonus is paid for a Subscription cancelled before it completes its first full paid month.

## 6. Clusters

- A cluster is 10 Subscriptions credited to the Rep that are active at the same time in the same neighborhood, each past its first full paid month. A Subscription counts toward one cluster only.
- For each cluster, Valet Network grants the Rep 1,500 shares of Valet Network, Inc. common stock under its equity incentive plan. Valet Network submits each grant to its board for approval within 30 days of the day the cluster is complete. The shares vest in full on the grant date and are subject to the plan and the award agreement issued with them.

## 7. Payment and statements

- Valet Network pays each month's commission and bonuses by the 10th day of the following month, by Zelle or ACH to the account the Rep gives Valet Network in writing.
- Each payment comes with a statement listing every Subscription credited to the Rep, its status that month and the amount it earned. The rep portal shows the same information at all times.
- Commission and bonuses already paid are not taken back, except an amount paid on a Subscription obtained through fraud or a breach of section 8.
- The Rep pays their own taxes and expenses. Valet Network issues an IRS Form 1099 for each year in which it pays the Rep $600 or more.

## 8. Conduct

- The Rep describes the service, its price and its terms only as Valet Network publishes them, and makes no other promise to a customer.
- The Rep does not disclose the Rep's commission to a customer.
- The Rep contacts only people who have agreed to be contacted, and follows the laws on calls, texts and email, including the Telephone Consumer Protection Act.
- The Rep does not sign up anyone without that person's consent, does not enter a payment card on a customer's behalf, and does not sign up a Subscription the customer does not intend to use.
- The Rep uses customer information only to sign customers up for Valet Network.

## 9. Ending this agreement

- Either party may end this agreement at any time by email to the other. It ends on the day the email is sent.
- After it ends, Valet Network keeps paying the commission and bonuses on every Subscription credited to the Rep before it ended, for as long as each stays active, under sections 3, 5 and 7.
- If the Rep commits fraud or materially breaches section 8, Valet Network may end this agreement by an email that states the breach. Valet Network then stops paying commission on the Subscriptions obtained through that breach. Commission on all other Subscriptions continues.

## 10. Independent contractor

- The Rep is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent of Valet Network, and receives no benefits from Valet Network.
- The Rep cannot bind Valet Network to any contract, price or promise.

## 11. Confidential information

- Valet Network's customer lists, target lists, unpublished pricing and the contents of the rep portal are confidential. The Rep uses them only under this agreement, does not share them, and deletes them when this agreement ends.

## 12. General

- New York law governs this agreement. The state and federal courts in New York County, New York, decide any dispute about it, and both parties consent to them.
- Notices are given by email: to Valet Network at rishi@valetnetwork.co, and to the Rep at the email above. Either party may change its address by notice.
- This is the whole agreement between the parties on its subject. It may be changed only in writing signed by both parties. Electronic signatures count.
- If any part of this agreement cannot be enforced, the rest still applies.
- The Rep signs electronically in the rep portal by typing their full name. Valet Network, Inc. signs by Rishi Ganesh, who issues this agreement in the portal. It binds both parties when the Rep signs, and each party receives a copy of the signed agreement by email.
