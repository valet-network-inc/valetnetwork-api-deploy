# Commission agreement

Between Valet Network, Inc. and an independent sales representative.

DRAFT — FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW

This agreement is between Valet Network, Inc., 21-31 24th Street, Astoria, NY 11105 ("Valet"), and {REP_NAME} ("the Rep"). It starts on {START_DATE}.

Plain English is used on purpose. It is written to be the written contract that New York City's Freelance Isn't Free Act and New York Labor Law section 191-c ask for: who the parties are, what the work is, how the pay is worked out, and when it is paid.

## 1. The work

The Rep finds customers in New York City for Valet's parking subscription and signs them up: buildings, brokers, businesses and individual users. A subscription is one drop-off and one pickup a day. Valet onboards the customer and runs the service. There is no base, quota, set hours, exclusivity or minimum number of subscriptions.

## 2. What Valet provides

The building packet, a target list of buildings, written answers to a customer's lawyer or insurance broker, inbound leads, and a personal code.

## 3. Pay

- A monthly commission for every subscription the Rep signed, for every month it is active. The amount depends on the customer's neighborhood ($50 in Brooklyn and Queens, up to $150 in Manhattan) and is listed at valetnetwork.co/pricing. It continues while the subscription is active, including after this agreement ends.
- $100 per subscription, earned when it completes its first full paid month. Free and pilot months do not count.
- Clusters. Ten of the Rep's subscriptions active at once in one neighborhood, each past its first full paid month, make a cluster; a subscription counts toward one. Each cluster earns 1,500 shares of common stock under the equity incentive plan when the tenth completes that month.
- Unlocks. A neighborhood unlocks at 10 subscribers with a card saved; they start 3 days later, and later subscribers start once their card is saved. No one is charged before then.
- No commission until the customer pays. There is no base pay, no fee to the Rep and nothing to buy.

## 4. Whose subscription it is

A subscription is the Rep's when the customer signs with the Rep's name on the agreement, or uses the Rep's code. A lead the Rep is working, or that Valet hands to the Rep, is the Rep's for 90 days from the first contact; no other rep is assigned to it in that time. Valet's records decide which rep a subscription belongs to.

## 5. When and how the Rep is paid

At the end of each month, for that month, by ACH or Zelle, with a statement that lists every subscription, its status that month, and the amount it earned. Customers are month to month and may cancel on 7 days' written notice. Commission ends on the last day paid for. No commission on credited time, no bonus if cancelled before the first full paid month, and paid commission is not taken back. The Rep tells Valet within 30 days of a statement if something on it is wrong.

## 6. Independent contractor

The Rep is an independent contractor, not an employee. The Rep pays their own taxes and expenses, gets no benefits, and receives an IRS Form 1099 for the year's pay. The Rep may work for others. The Rep cannot bind Valet: the price is set by Valet for the customer's neighborhood and stated in the customer's agreement, and nothing outside the packet is promised to a customer.

## 7. Conduct

The Rep does not change the price or the customer agreement, and never discloses the Rep's commission to a customer, in writing or on a call. The Rep does not misstate what the service does, what it costs or what it covers. The Rep follows the packet and the rep guide.

## 8. Confidential information

Valet's pricing, customer and target lists, and the contents of the packet are confidential. The Rep uses them only to sell for Valet and returns or deletes them when this agreement ends.

## 9. Ending it

Either side may end this agreement at any time by written notice. Pay already earned is still paid, and section 3's monthly commission keeps paying while each of the Rep's subscriptions stays active.

## 10. General

- Governing law. New York law governs this agreement. Venue: New York County, New York.
- Notices. In writing, to Valet at 21-31 24th Street, Astoria, NY 11105 or rishi@valetnetwork.co, and to the Rep at the email above.
- Whole agreement. This is the whole agreement on the subject. Changes must be in writing and signed by both sides.
- Severability. If one part cannot be enforced, the rest still stands.
- Counterparts. It may be signed in counterparts, including electronically.
